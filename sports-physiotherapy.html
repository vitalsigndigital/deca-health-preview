What is sports physiotherapy?

Whether you're a weekend warrior or a competitive athlete, sports physiotherapy is designed around the demands of your sport. Our team — including a Certified Athletic Therapist — combines acute injury management, functional movement assessment and return-to-play strategies.

We don't just get you out of pain; we build resilience so you're less likely to be re-injured. Expect a proactive, hands-on plan tailored to your goals, your position and your season.

Conditions we treat

Sports physiotherapy is ideal for athletes and active people dealing with:

  • Sport-specific return-to-play strategies
