Care built around you — not a conveyor belt

DECA Health and Wellness is a collaborative, patient-centered clinic in Oshawa. We combine physiotherapy, chiropractic and registered massage therapy to help you move better, feel stronger and live pain-free.

Our Story

A modern clinic with an old-fashioned focus on people

We built DECA because too many people were being rushed through crowded clinics with long wait times and little real attention. We wanted something different: a clean, modern space where you get unhurried, one-on-one care from clinicians who take the time to understand the why behind your pain.

Conveniently located just one minute off the Highway 407 exit, our clinic makes getting expert care quick and stress-free. From your first assessment to your last session, our goal is simple — get you back to the activities you love, as efficiently as possible.

Why Patients Choose Us

What makes DECA different

Every decision we make is designed to help you heal faster and feel genuinely cared for.

Fast Access
