What is physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is a hands-on, evidence-based approach to assessing, diagnosing and treating pain, injury and movement problems. Rather than just chasing symptoms, our physiotherapists identify the true root cause so your recovery is complete and lasting.

At DECA, every plan starts with a detailed assessment of your movement, strength and history. From there we combine manual therapy, targeted exercise and education to help you heal — often with meaningful relief in as few as two to three visits.
