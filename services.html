Home Services

Expert care for pain, injury and performance

Six complementary services, one collaborative team. Whether you're recovering from an injury or optimizing your performance, we'll match you with the right treatment plan.

Physiotherapy

Comprehensive assessment, manual therapy, corrective exercise and modalities to treat the root cause of your pain and restore movement.

Learn more

Sports Physiotherapy

Recover faster, prevent re-injury and return to play stronger with functional rehab and performance-focused programming.

Learn more

Pelvic Floor Physiotherapy

Discreet, specialized care for pelvic health, incontinence, core stability and post-natal recovery in a comfortable setting.

Learn more

Shockwave Therapy

Non-invasive acoustic wave therapy that stimulates healing and breaks down scar tissue for chronic tendon and heel pain.

Learn more

Chiropractic

Precise spinal adjustments and soft-tissue therapy to reduce pain, improve alignment and restore healthy joint function.

Learn more

Massage Therapy

Registered massage therapy including deep tissue, trigger point and myofascial release to relieve tension and support recovery.

Learn more

Acupuncture

Treatment with a Registered Acupuncturist for pain, tension, headaches, sleep and stress — on its own or alongside your rehab plan.

Learn more
