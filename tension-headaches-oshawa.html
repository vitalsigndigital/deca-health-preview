What are tension headaches?

Tension headaches are the most common type of headache — often felt as a band of pressure around the head. Many are closely linked to tension in the neck and upper shoulders, posture and stress. When the neck is the driver, they’re called cervicogenic headaches, and physiotherapy can be especially effective.

By treating the neck and the habits behind your headaches, we help many patients experience fewer, less intense headaches — without relying on medication.

Common causes of tension headaches

  • Neck and upper-shoulder muscle tension
  • Poor posture and ‘tech neck’
  • Stress and jaw tension
  • Prolonged sitting and screen time
  • Neck joint stiffness or dysfunction

Symptoms we treat

  • A band-like pressure or tightness around the head
  • Pain spreading from the neck or base of the skull
  • Tight, tender neck and shoulder muscles
  • Headaches that worsen with stress or long screen time
  • Recurring or frequent headaches

How we treat tension headaches in Oshawa

We target the neck and the causes of your headaches, which may include:

  • Manual therapy for the neck and upper back
  • Trigger-point and massage therapy
  • Postural correction and ergonomic advice
  • Strengthening for the neck and upper back
  • Stress and habit management strategies
  • Acupuncture where appropriate

Care is led by our physiotherapy and massage therapy teams.

What to expect

Many people see a real reduction in headache frequency and intensity once the neck is treated and posture is addressed. Please note: sudden, severe or unusual headaches should be assessed by a doctor — we’ll help you know the difference.
