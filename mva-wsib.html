Auto Injury (MVA)

Recover fully after a motor vehicle accident

If you’ve been in a car accident, early, coordinated rehabilitation makes a real difference. Our team creates a personalized plan that combines physiotherapy, chiropractic and massage therapy to relieve pain, restore movement and prevent long-term problems — while we handle the paperwork.

Injuries we commonly treat

  • Whiplash & neck injuries
  • Back pain & sciatica
  • Shoulder injuries
  • Concussions
  • Muscle strains & sprains
  • Joint stiffness
  • Soft-tissue injuries
  • Chronic post-accident pain
