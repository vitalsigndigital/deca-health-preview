What is shoulder pain?

The shoulder is the most mobile joint in the body, which also makes it prone to injury and stiffness. Shoulder pain can come from the rotator cuff, tendons, bursa or joint capsule, and often makes everyday movements — reaching, lifting, dressing or sleeping — painful.

We assess exactly which structures are involved and why, then treat the cause with hands-on therapy and a progressive strengthening plan to restore pain-free movement.

Common causes of shoulder pain

  • Rotator cuff strain or tear
  • Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis)
  • Shoulder impingement
  • Bursitis or tendonitis
  • Overuse from sport, work or repetitive lifting
  • Post-surgery stiffness
  • Poor posture

Symptoms we treat

  • Pain when lifting or reaching overhead
  • Stiffness and limited range of motion
  • Night pain or trouble sleeping on the shoulder
  • Weakness in the arm
  • Clicking or catching
  • Pain radiating down the arm

How we treat shoulder pain in Oshawa

Your plan targets the specific cause of your shoulder pain and may include:

  • Manual therapy and joint mobilization
  • Rotator cuff and scapular strengthening
  • Shockwave therapy for stubborn tendon pain
  • Soft-tissue and massage therapy
  • Post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Activity modification and education

Depending on your needs, care may involve our physiotherapy, shockwave therapy and massage therapy teams.

What to expect

Recovery depends on the cause — many rotator cuff and impingement problems improve steadily with hands-on care and strengthening, while frozen shoulder can take longer but responds well to guided therapy. We’ll set clear milestones and progress your plan as you improve.

Frequently asked questions about shoulder pain

Rotator cuff problems typically cause pain when lifting or reaching, weakness, and night pain. A physiotherapy assessment can identify the injury and its severity without needing an immediate scan.
Frozen shoulder can be slow, but guided physiotherapy speeds recovery by maintaining and restoring movement through each stage. Many people see steady improvement over weeks to months.
Most shoulder pain — including many rotator cuff and impingement problems — improves with conservative care. We also provide pre- and post-surgical rehab when surgery is needed.
Shockwave uses acoustic waves to stimulate healing in stubborn tendon problems such as calcific tendonitis. It’s often combined with physiotherapy for the best results.
Physiotherapy, chiropractic and massage therapy for this condition are covered by most extended-health plans, and we direct bill. We also handle motor vehicle accident (MVA) and WSIB claims.
